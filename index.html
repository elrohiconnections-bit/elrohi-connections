ABOUT ELROHI 01

WHO WE ARE

Helping people grow from the inside out.

ELROHI Connection is a faith-based mentoring and development initiative dedicated to helping young people and families grow in emotional health, purpose, leadership, and practical life skills.

Through structured mentoring programs, youth education initiatives, and family support frameworks, we work to build strong individuals, stable homes, and responsible future leaders.

WHAT WE DO

Youth Emotional & Purpose Mentoring

  • Leadership development
  • Purpose discovery

02

STEM With Purpose

A structured 10-week youth development program integrating science, technology, engineering and mathematics with ethical responsibility and purpose discovery.

  • Problem solving
  • Responsible innovation
  • Team collaboration
  • Career exploration

Single Mothers & Single Fathers Support

An 8-week support and empowerment framework helping single parents gain emotional strength, parenting confidence, and community support.

  • Emotional healing
  • Parenting guidance
  • Faith and resilience
  • Family stability

About the Founder

“Growth becomes sustainable when people are seen, strengthened and guided toward purpose.”

Johnson Onibokun is a pastor, youth mentor, and educator with over 30 years of experience working with secondary school students, young adults, and families.

His work focuses on helping individuals address the deeper roots of emotional struggles while guiding them toward personal growth, leadership development, and purposeful living.
